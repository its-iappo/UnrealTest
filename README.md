# The Crimson Collapse

The kingdom of Aetherfall is dying.

Once protected by ancient arcane engines and sacred runes woven into the land itself, the realm has now fallen into chaos. The skies burn crimson. The forests no longer breathe. Rivers carry ash instead of water, and every living creature whispers the same warning:

> "The Core is broken."

Deep beneath the capital lies an abandoned creation forged by the Archmages of the Old Era: a fragmented world of unstable magic, corrupted mechanisms, and unfinished rituals. What once maintained balance across the realm has become dangerously inconsistent.

Spells fail without reason. Ancient devices no longer respond. Victory seals remain dormant even after their trials are completed. Entire sections of the realm collapse under contradictory laws of magic.

And worse still...

The Great Compilation has failed.

The kingdom now calls upon a rare order of adventurers known as the Debuggers: warriors capable of entering broken systems, deciphering corrupted logic, and restoring stability where reality itself refuses to function.

Your mission is simple:

* Investigate the corrupted systems
* Hunt the hidden flaws scattered across the realm
* Repair the broken mechanisms hidden in the ancient runes of the Codex of Primordial Programming
* Restore the world before the atmosphere becomes completely uninhabitable

Beware:
Not every failure is visible.
Some bugs hide deep within the foundations of the world itself.

Welcome to the Bug Bounty of Aetherfall.
